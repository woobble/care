## Verification record

- The expanded Authentik suite completed 12/12 tests in 4.9 minutes on the isolated stack. An earlier local Authentik navigation timeout passed in a focused retry after the test navigation wait was adjusted (1/1, 1.6 minutes). Settings passed in the full run (16.5 seconds), and the identity journey emitted the performance observations recorded in the Goal 13 report. The browser coverage included admission/planning, service/client/benefit/agreement configuration, caregiver execution, evidence and unsent-draft recovery, billing/payment, documents/communications, identity and facility scope administration, Operations tasks and recurrence, responsive shell/theme, caregiver Today, absence-to-substitution access, and settings save/error states. The identity/access journey temporarily adds its test administrator to inspect the pilot and cleans up that membership. Most E2E records remain in the separate local Demo Care tenant.
- Read-only reconciliation of the live-date database found 2 facilities, 10 employees, 20 clients, 6 services, 20 agreements, 7 funding sources, 120 visits, 1 completed execution/evidence package, 2 €17 invoices, 1 accepted local €17 claim, €22.66 payments, and 15/15 source-linked Operations tasks. The rerun reported `generated=0` and `already-existing=120`. The payer invoice is fully paid; the client invoice has €5.66 paid and €11.34 outstanding. A separate retrospective database has 120 visits, 4 weekly completed execution/document/evidence packages, one allocated service, two €18 invoices, an accepted local €18 claim, and €24 in recorded payments/payment allocations. Three other completed services remain unallocated and uninvoiced.
- API/PostgreSQL integration passed: 6 tests and 1,335 assertions across tenant scoping, benefit allocations and reversals, expired carryover and exhausted provisional balance handling, billing history and invoice correction, partial acceptance and full rejection with corrected resubmission, documents, Operations source scope, overdue receivable and onboarding follow-up visibility, dashboard counts, and communications/onboarding. Worker outbox and database RLS/migration integration tasks passed in the same full command.
- Settings browser coverage saved billing profile, payer directory and requirements, document template, and agreement reminder values, reloaded each, then exercised validation or stale-version errors. The focused regression confirmed attempted values remain after recoverable errors, no stale success status appears, and stored values remain unchanged after reload. A stale document-template conflict previously appeared as “Logo uploaded”; the corrected error route is covered (PILOT-008).
- Focused caregiver and settings regressions resolved PILOT-006 and PILOT-007. The caregiver regression returned a synthetic 503, confirmed no server entry, reloaded, and restored the same local draft. A retrospective run completed one visit in each of four simulated weeks, and the billing integration journey covers full rejection followed by corrected resubmission. Benefits integration verified §45a external-input gating and provisional allocation with a zero confirmed balance, persisted §45b carryover available through June 30 and excluded on July 1, rejection of allocation through an expired entitlement when a current entitlement exists, and exhaustion of a provider-reported balance without reporting confirmed funds. Operations integration covers overdue invoice and onboarding follow-up visibility, rejected-claim and missing-evidence work items, dashboard counts, unassigned visits, and agreement, qualification, benefit, payer-authorization, and onboarding deadlines. The benefits-billing browser journey confirms an unallocated completed service appears in Operations and clears after funding allocation. Two concurrent administrator scope replacements serialized to one complete submitted scope set; the service currently uses last-write-wins without a version-conflict response. `PILOT-005` remains a low-priority hydration warning from an earlier local journey. The persistent pilot visit intentionally has no client acknowledgement; acknowledgement, decline, and correction states were exercised in the separate caregiver journey. Remaining gaps include realistic daily execution volume, three completed services without allocations/invoices, and load/query-profile testing. Final repository gate results and remaining gaps are in [pilot-goal-13-report.md](pilot-goal-13-report.md).
