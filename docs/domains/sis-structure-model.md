# SIS® and the Structure Model boundary

## Purpose and current boundary

Care Platform's care-planning foundation is a generic domain for person perspective, professional assessment, needs, resources, planning responses to risks, goals, measures, and evaluation. It may align with the broad stages of the German care process, but it is not the official SIS® and must not be described as an SIS implementation, reproduction, equivalent, or compliant form.

No authorized official SIS artifacts or technical requirements have been supplied to or used for this foundation. It does not include official SIS wording, form fields, topic structure, layout, graphics, workflow, or technical schema. The generic model must not acquire SIS-specific fields or labels by approximation.

## Compliance constraint

Official materials, content, and technical requirements must come from an authorized source and be used only under the applicable license and review. The SIS® name and marks must not imply endorsement or conformance. Before building or distributing an official representation, the project must establish the authorized version, usage rights, required behavior and technical format, and the necessary legal/compliance review. Without those inputs, the project does not reproduce the instrument or claim official compatibility.

## Future adapter boundary

An official integration belongs in a separately owned, versioned adapter or representation module. It must keep the authorized official representation, its version metadata, validation, and any required rendering or exchange format isolated from the generic care-planning aggregate. The adapter may link to generic care-plan concepts, but it must not redefine those concepts as official SIS fields or make generic care plans depend on proprietary representation details.

Any mapping between an official representation and the generic domain must be explicit, version-aware, reviewed, and preserve provenance. Only mappings supported by the authorized requirements should be made; unmapped or lossy data must remain visible as such rather than being silently discarded or inferred. The adapter boundary does not itself grant rights to official materials or establish conformance.

The adapter is a future boundary, not a current feature. Its exact data contract, screens, import/export behavior, lifecycle, retention, and permissions must be decided from the authorized requirements when those are available. Until then, the generic care-planning domain remains independently usable and carries no official SIS label.

## What the generic model is not

- It is not the official SIS® form, a fragment of that form, or an unofficial approximation of its topics.
- It does not reproduce official questions, wording, structure, visual design, or completion workflow.
- It does not claim legal authorization, endorsement, certification, or conformance.
- Its high-level care-process compatibility does not make its fields or assessments equivalent to official SIS content.
